# Türsteher Berlin

A tiny Pokémon-style pixel game about getting into Berlin clubs. Walk around a night map of Berlin (Spree, Oberbaumbrücke, East Side Gallery, Fernsehturm, Ostkreuz) and answer the bouncer's questions at eight real clubs:

Berghain · Tresor · KitKatClub · RSO · Kater Blau · Sisyphos · Club der Visionäre · Heideglühen

A new game starts with a short intro: choose man, woman or non-binary, then one of four starting characters (the Tourist, the Hipster, the Tech Guy/Girl/Engineer, the Local or the Art Student). Each starts in different clothes, so each has a different first route through the clubs.

Every bouncer checks your outfit first, then asks questions about the club. Get every question right and you're in. One wrong answer and it's *Heute nicht.*

## Inside Berghain

Once the bouncer lets you in, you can walk around inside Berghain. It has three floors connected by steel stairs, following the real layout:

- **Erdgeschoss (ground floor):** ticket desk, coat check, the Piotr Nathan wall installation, a bar and a darkroom.
- **Main floor (old turbine hall):** dancefloor, seven Funktion-One stacks, DJ booth, two bars, the Eisbar on the mezzanine and the toilets.
- **Panorama Bar:** wrap-around bar, raised DJ booth, a Wolfgang Tillmans print and tall windows with metal blinds.

Side quests (tracked on the stamp card):

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Check your coat | Garderobe, ground floor |
| Order a drink | Any bar |
| Get ice cream | Eisbar, main floor mezzanine |
| Dance on the main floor | Press A on the dancefloor |
| Get into the toilets | A guy blocks the toilet door and wants K. He sends you to Mila (pink hair, sunglasses, by the left speakers on the main dancefloor; say he sent you). Bring the bag back and you share a line in a stall: 10 seconds of melting, blurry, colour-shifting slow motion |
| Take someone to the darkroom | Find Sascha at Panorama Bar, ask first, then lead them down to the ground floor |
| Dance when the blinds open | After three other quests, go to Panorama Bar |

Leave through the exit on the ground floor. Other clubs still show the short "inside" scene.

## Inside Sisyphos

An old dog-biscuit factory in Rummelsburg. You arrive in the garden and walk through doorways (bead curtains) between rooms:

- **Strand:** the open-air garden. Sand dancefloor, a pool with sand around it, art totems, fairy lights, a bar.
- **Wintergarten:** a glass house full of plants. House music that never stops.
- **Hammahalle:** the brick factory hall. Techno.
- **Dampfer:** wooden, ship-like, with portholes. Deep house.
- **Tunnel:** a long techno corridor with red lacquer walls and swinging paper lanterns.
- **Sauna:** behind the Dampfer, with a Saunameister doing the Aufguss.

The crowd is a colourful festival and circus bunch with flower crowns, glitter and face paint. Talk to them for stories about yoga retreats, ayahuasca and van life.

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Pfand hunter (main quest) | Empty bottles lie around every room. Carry up to 4, return them at any bar for the deposit (beer 8 ct, Club-Mate 15 ct, cans 25 ct). Return 8 to finish |
| Jump in the pool | Press A facing the pool in the garden |
| Survive the Aufguss | Talk to the Saunameister |
| Listen to 3 hippie stories | Momo (pool), Luna (Wintergarten), Kai (sauna), Sunny (Tunnel) |
| Learn to juggle | Zora the circus kid in the garden. Wait for the beat |
| Dance in 4 different rooms | Press A on any dancefloor: sand, wood, concrete or the red Tunnel floor |
| Ringbahn rush | Ronny on the Dampfer wants his bucket hat back (the raver at the pool found it). His pill turns the game into a 30-second 3D runner through an S-Bahn: swipe or the joystick to switch tracks, A or up to jump over bikes and Sterni crates, dodge the Kontrolleure and poles, grab water for an extra heart |

## Inside KitKat

KitKat shares its industrial building on Köpenicker Straße with Sage Club. In the game it has three areas:

- **Main floor:** UV light and fluorescent wall paintings, a pole stage, leather sofas, a love swing, the Garderobe and a dark corner.
- **Pool lounge:** behind the curtains on the right. A warm pool, beds, and the Awareness Station at the pool bar.
- **Dungeon:** downstairs, under red light. St Andrew's crosses, cages, the gynaecologist's chair, two darkrooms, Marco from Napoli and a photo booth.

The crowd wears latex, leather, harnesses, bunny ears or nothing much. Groups on beds and in the darkrooms are shown, never spelled out: bodies under a blanket, rocking, little hearts. Talk to a group and ask to join. Some say yes, some are a closed group, some are too busy to answer.

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Strip down at the Garderobe | By the entrance. You're in underwear for as long as you stay inside |
| Spin on a pole | Pole stage, top of the main floor |
| Take a dip in the pool | Pool lounge |
| Join an orgy | Ask a group that says yes (dark corner on the main floor, a bed by the pool, the big darkroom in the dungeon) |
| Say yes to Marco | Marco asks to spank you. Yes, gently or no: all answers are respected |
| Photo booth souvenir | After Marco, the booth in the dungeon prints a 4-frame strip. "Save photo" downloads it as a PNG |

## Inside RSO

RSO (Revier Südost) is run by the crew behind Griessmühle, a much-loved Neukölln club in an old pasta factory on the canal that had to close in February 2020 when its lease ran out (after protests and a petition with more than 50,000 signatures). The crew started again the same year in the old Bärenquell brewery in Schöneweide. In the game:

- **OPAN:** the open-air floor in the brewery yard, with fairy lights, a bar and Kalle, an old Griessmühle regular.
- **ROBUS** and **SUMME:** the two warehouse floors.
- **Keller:** a dark space under ROBUS. This one is made up for the game; no source mentions a darkroom at RSO.

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Dance on all three floors | ROBUS, SUMME and OPAN |
| Hear about Griessmühle | Kalle in the OPAN yard |
| A drink in the old brewery | Any bar |
| The Keller | Downstairs from ROBUS. Ask first |

People inside clubs who start a mini game have a red **!** over their head (the toilet guy at Berghain, Ronny on the Sisyphos Dampfer); it disappears once you've done it.

## Inside Club der Visionäre

CDV is a former boathouse on the Flutgraben, a flood channel off the Spree where Kreuzberg meets Alt-Treptow, started around 2001 by Gregor Kraemer (who comes from jazz). One small wooden dancefloor on the water, partly roofed, under a big weeping willow; the Sonnenraum is the little indoor room. It's a home of minimal (Ricardo Villalobos, Zip, Sonja Moonear, Sammy Dee have all played), the bar is cash only, on busy summer weekends it can run from Friday to Monday, and in winter the parties move to the Hoppetosse, its sister ship nearby (a steamer from 1928). A fire took part of the wooden outdoor area on 15 June 2019, and the regulars helped save the club with the Save CDV crowdfunding in 2020.

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Cash only | The bar refuses your card. Ines on the bench lends you 10 € |
| Under the willow | Press A facing the weeping willow |
| See you Monday | Dance on the deck three times: Saturday, Sunday, Monday morning |
| The new planks | Tom by the lighter boards tells you about the 2019 fire |
| The early days | Ines, a regular since the start |
| Jazz in the Sonnenraum | The bassist of the trio needs someone on the hi-hat |

## Inside Heideglühen

Heideglühen started in 2011 in a potato garden on Heidestraße and has been at the Westhafen since 2012: mostly open air, fire pits, light installations, a wooden dancefloor under a glass roof, and mostly house music (it has its own label, Heideglühen Tonträger). On the game map it sits in the park in the south-east.

The night has its own clock: it starts at 03:30 and moves on as you dance, sing and sit by the fire.

| Quest | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Back to back at 5am (main quest) | Ask the DJ under the glass roof. He sends you to Woody; once Woody says yes, come back at 05:00 and pick the right records for a three-track set |
| Get Woody's yes | Woody, the booker: bald, in the scene for decades, usually by the fire |
| Karaoke in the toilets | The karaoke machine between the sinks |
| Warm up by the fire | Press A facing a fire pit |
| Get a drink | The bar by the fence |

Woody, the DJ and the karaoke are inventions for the game.

## The guest list

A new game asks for your name (it doesn't have to be unique; each player gets a short ID like `#a3f9`). The **guest list** (G, the button on the stamp card or title screen, or the Litfaßsäule next to the Fernsehturm, labelled GUEST LIST on the map) shows everyone who has played: who finished (fastest first, by active play time: time with the game open and in front), who is still partying (clubs and quests so far), and who just entered a name. It is shared and permanent, stored in Firebase (project `berlin-club-game`, Firestore in Frankfurt). Start over makes a new entry.

## The ending

Get a stamp from every club and the night finally catches up with you: you wake up in your childhood bedroom back home (old DJ posters, the controller from year one, an alarm clock at 9:12). Congratulations, you completed Berlin. The game then sums up your seven years, using what you actually did (your Pfand earnings, that one back to back at Heideglühen). Afterwards you can go back to Berlin or start over.

## Music

All music is generated in the browser while you play (Web Audio, no audio files). Every club floor has its own style, based on what each club is known for:

| Where | Style |
| --- | --- |
| The street | Soft ambient pads, a few bells, a little wind |
| Berghain main floor | Dark, hypnotic techno with a rumbling kick (heard through the concrete on the ground floor) |
| Panorama Bar | House with piano stabs and a shuffle |
| Tresor | Detroit-rooted techno with minor-chord stabs |
| KitKat | Disco-tinged electro; disco house by the pool; EBM-style techno in the dungeon |
| RSO | Hypnotic deep techno in ROBUS, dub techno in SUMME, melodic techno on OPAN (muffled in the Keller) |
| Kater Blau | Bouncy tech house |
| Sisyphos | Organic house with marimba and hand drums in the garden, deep house in the Wintergarten, techno in the Hammahalle, deep tech house on the Dampfer, acid techno in the Tunnel, ambient in the sauna |
| Club der Visionäre | Clicky, sparse minimal on the deck; a jazz trio (swung ride, walking bass, piano) in the Sonnenraum |
| Heideglühen | Warm classic house with piano chords (heard through the door in the toilets) |

Music starts with your first click or key press (browsers require that). Styles live in `src/audio/styles.ts`: tempo, 16-step drum patterns, bass line, chords and which sounds play.

## Development

The game is TypeScript, built with [Vite](https://vite.dev). You need Node.js 18 or newer.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the URL it prints (http://localhost:5173). The page reloads as you save.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `npm run dev` | Local dev server with live reload |
| `npm run typecheck` | Type-checks the code (strict mode) |
| `npm run build` | Type-checks, then builds the site into `dist/` |
| `npm run preview` | Serves the built `dist/` locally |

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Arrow keys / WASD | Joystick (slide past its rim to run) |
| Talk / confirm | Z / Enter / Space | A |
| Skip text | X / Esc | B |
| Run | hold Tab (or Shift) | hold B, or drag far from where your finger started |
| Zoom | + / − (or mouse wheel over the map) | + / − buttons on the screen |
| Whole map | M | MAP button |
| Sound on/off | N | ♪ button on the screen |
| Guest list | G | Guest list button on the stamp card or the title screen |

Zoomed out, the map labels every club (✓ once you've been in) and shop, and marks where you are. The zoom level is saved.

On phones the page switches to a full-screen layout: drag anywhere on the map to walk (like a joystick), tap the map or the text box to talk, or use the joystick (hold and slide your thumb, no need to lift it to turn; past the rim you run) and the A/B buttons. The stamp card opens from the button in the top bar. Landscape puts the joystick left and A/B right of the map. The game is drawn at the screen's full resolution, so pixels stay sharp on high-density phones. Club and shop names only show in the MAP view on phones. Tip: "Add to Home Screen" runs it full-screen.

Progress (stamps, position) is saved in `localStorage`. "Start over" on the stamp card clears it.

## Editing the game

- **Clubs and questions**: `CLUB_DEFS` in `src/data/clubs.ts`. Each club has `greet`, `reject`, `accept`, `inside`, `sign` lines, `need` (how many questions it asks) and a `pool` of questions. In each question the **first** answer in `a` is the correct one; options get shuffled in game. `fact` is shown after a wrong answer.
- **Map layout**: `buildCity()` in `src/world/city.ts`. Roads come from `hroad` / `vroad`, buildings from `building(...)`, and trees from the coordinate list. Club position comes from each club's `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`. The door sits at the bottom centre, the bouncer one tile below it.
- **Outfits, dress codes, shops**: `OUTFITS`, `DRESS` and `SHOP_DEFS` in `src/data/outfits.ts`. Each outfit has `tags` (`black`, `latex`, `colorful`, `vintage`, `tourist`, `suit`, `tech`). A club's `ok` list needs at least one matching tag; its `no` list rejects any match.
- **Starting characters**: `CHARACTERS` in `src/data/outfits.ts`. `body` (skin, hair, `long` hair, `glasses`, `beard`) stays when you change clothes; `outfit` is the starting outfit.
- **Fernsehturm**: `TOWER` in `src/world/city.ts`. The base is solid; the tall part is drawn in front of the sprites.
- **Club interiors**: `src/data/interiors.ts`. Each floor is ASCII art (legend at the top of the file), plus spawn points, which floor the stairs lead to, the people on that floor (`lines` for chat, or `act` for a quest handler in `src/quests/acts.ts`), how many dancers to scatter, tile descriptions (`examine`) and the quest list. To give another club an interior, add an entry with the club's id (e.g. `tresor`) and its handlers. TypeScript checks the data against `InteriorDef` in `src/types.ts`, so typos in field names show up in `npm run typecheck`.
- **People on the street**: one entry each in `src/data/npcs.ts` (id, position, look, `lines`). Add `leave: 'left'` or `'right'` for someone who walks off after talking and stays gone (Start over brings them back). For custom behaviour, set `act` and write a handler in `STREET_ACTS` in `src/world/street.ts`. The `btc` look option prints a ₿ on their top.
- **Buildings you can walk into** (no bouncer, no stamp), like Antonio's hair salon: an entry in `src/data/places.ts` (position, sign) plus an `INTERIORS` entry with the same id in `src/data/interiors.ts` for the inside. Handlers for its people go in `src/places/` and are listed in `ACTS`. Haircuts are in `src/data/hair.ts`.
- **Signs on the street**: `cityPeople()` in `src/world/city.ts`.
- **Intro / ending text**: `INTRO` and `FINALE` in `src/data/clubs.ts`.

All graphics are drawn in code on a 384×256 canvas (16 px tiles, 24×16 visible). The view size is set in `src/core/runtime.ts` (`VW`/`VH`) and `fitCanvas()` in `src/render/canvas.ts`. There are no image assets.

## Deploy

Every push to `main` runs `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`: it type-checks, builds, and publishes `dist/` to GitHub Pages (about a minute). The repo's **Settings → Pages → Source** must be set to **GitHub Actions**.

## Monitoring

The live site reports to Datadog RUM (`src/core/monitoring.ts`): sessions, errors, and game events sent with `track()`, e.g. `door_rejected` (club, reason), `door_accepted`, `quest_completed`, `club_entered`, `npc_talked`, `outfit_changed`, `character_selected`, `game_finished`. Only production builds report: `npm run dev` sends nothing, and `npm run preview` reports as `env:local` so test sessions are easy to filter out. Sample rates and privacy are changed in Datadog (remote configuration), no deploy needed.

To track something new, call `track('snake_case_name', { key: 'value' })` where it happens.

## Debugging

Open the page with `?debug` (e.g. `http://localhost:5173/?debug`) and inspect `window.__game` in the browser console for the player position and game state. `__game.tp(x, y, 'up')` teleports, `__game.enterClub('berghain')` puts you inside.
